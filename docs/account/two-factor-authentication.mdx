---
title: "Two-factor authentication"
description: "Protect your account with a code from an authenticator app, as well as your password."
---

**Two-factor authentication (2FA)** means signing in needs two things: your password, and a short code from an app on your phone that changes every 30 seconds. Even if someone learns your password, they can't sign in without your phone.

Because a flitops account can reach your servers, we strongly recommend turning 2FA on.

## What you need

An authenticator app that supports **TOTP** (time-based one-time passwords), for example 1Password, Bitwarden, Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or Authy.

## Turning it on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the 2FA settings">
    Go to **Settings → Two-Factor Auth** and click **Enable 2FA**. You may be asked to confirm your password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add flitops to your authenticator app">
    Scan the QR code with your app, or type in the setup key shown underneath if you can't scan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with a code">
    Enter the 6-digit code your app shows and click **Confirm**. 2FA is now on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your recovery codes">
    flitops shows a list of **recovery codes**. Each one lets you sign in once if you lose your phone. Store them somewhere safe, such as your password manager. You can view them again or click **Regenerate Codes** later from the same page (regenerating makes the old ones stop working).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Signing in with 2FA

After entering your password, you'll be asked for the **authentication code** from your app. If you don't have your phone, choose to enter a **recovery code** instead. Each recovery code works only once.

## Turning it off

Go to **Settings → Two-Factor Auth** and click **Disable 2FA**. We don't recommend this unless you're about to set it up again on a new device.

<Warning>If you lose both your phone and your recovery codes, you won't be able to sign in. Keep your recovery codes somewhere you can always reach.</Warning>
