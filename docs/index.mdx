description: "flitops sets up servers on your own cloud account, deploys your code to them, and keeps them running."
---

flitops is a server management and deployment platform. You connect a cloud account (or bring a server you already have), and flitops installs everything your application needs: the web server, PHP, a database, Redis, Node.js and a process manager. From then on you deploy your code with a click, and flitops handles domains, SSL certificates, background workers, scheduled jobs, backups and monitoring.

The servers are yours. They live in your own cloud account, you pay your provider for them directly, and your application traffic and data go straight to them. flitops never sits in the middle of your users' requests.
