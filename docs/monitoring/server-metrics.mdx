Each server's **Overview** shows three numbers that tell you whether it's healthy:

| Metric | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **CPU load** | How busy the processor is. Consistently high load means requests queue up and your site slows down. |
| **Memory usage** | How much RAM is in use. When memory runs out, the server starts using slow swap space, and programs may be stopped. |
| **Disk usage** | How full the disk is. A full disk breaks deploys, backups, logs and databases. |

## How often they update

flitops collects metrics from every active server **every 10 minutes**. Click **Refresh** on the server's overview to collect them right now; the numbers update live when they arrive.

## What to do when numbers are high

- **CPU load high for a long time**: look for a runaway worker or cron job on the [Processes](/servers/processes) tab, or upgrade to a size with more vCPUs at your provider.
- **Memory high**: reduce the number of worker processes (**Numprocs**), lower PHP's memory limit, or upgrade to a size with more RAM.
- **Disk nearly full**: clear old logs, check large uploads, and make sure [backups](/backups/overview) go to your storage bucket (they do by default). Consider a larger disk.

<Note>Metrics are a snapshot every 10 minutes, so short spikes between checks may not show. More detailed graphs, history and alerts on high usage are on the [roadmap](/roadmap).</Note>
