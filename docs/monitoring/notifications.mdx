flitops tells you when something needs your attention, by email and, for some failures, with a banner in the dashboard.

## Emails

| Email | Sent when | Sent to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Server provisioning failed** | Creating or provisioning a server fails | The person who created the server |
| **Site is down** | A site fails 2 uptime checks in a row | The team owner |
| **Site recovered** | A site that was down is reachable again | The team owner |
| **SSL certificate expiring** | A served certificate is 14, 7, 3, 1 or 0 days from expiry | The team owner |
| **SSL certificate request failed** | Issuing or renewing a certificate for a domain fails | The team owner |
| **Database backup failed** | A backup fails | The person who started it, or the team owner for scheduled backups |
| **Team invitation** | Someone invites you to a team | The invited email address |

Account emails (verifying your email address and resetting your password) are sent to you as needed.

## Dashboard alerts

Failures that need action, such as a **failed backup**, also appear as a banner at the top of every page until you dismiss them. Click **View details** to go to the problem, or **Dismiss** to hide it.

## Live updates

You don't need to refresh the page to see progress. Deployments, provisioning, backups, domain verification, worker status, metrics and more update live while you watch.

## Making sure you get emails

- Add flitops's sending address to your contacts, or allow it in your spam filter.
- Most alerts go to the team owner (the person who created the team), so make sure that account uses an email address someone reads.

<Note>Choosing who receives each alert, and sending alerts to Slack, SMS or webhooks, is on the [roadmap](/roadmap).</Note>
